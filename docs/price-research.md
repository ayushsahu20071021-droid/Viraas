Checked: 2026-09-23 (UTC). Sources were accessible web search extracts, not checkout verification. Search-index prices may be stale, region-specific or conditional. No item is promoted to LIVE.

## Method

The catalog is original editorial styling, NOT an exact-SKU feed. Unverified legacy brand claims and synthetic MRPs were removed. Exact marketplace brands below identify only the observed comparator. Products carry CHECK, a price basis and evidence ID. A merchant search URL is a discovery fallback, not proof of an identical purchasable garment.

Estimates use the documented family range: simple/printed construction at the lower end, threadwork in the middle, mirror/bead embellishment at the upper end. A heavy handwork or pure-silk claim is not substantiated by a basic machine-embroidered comparator. There is no fabricated discount or commission.

## Coverage and limitations

- Family-comparable estimates: **484/653**. Not exact SKU matches.
- **169/653 still require appropriate product-family evidence** (including embroidered ethnic shirts and accessories/beauty families). Their inherited prices remain explicitly unverified; no false mapping to an unrelated comparator.
- Approved sources consulted: Myntra, AJIO, Flipkart, Meesho, Nykaa; targeted Shopsy searches returned unrelated sites and were excluded.
- Nykaa is restricted to beauty in the catalog. Kajal research showed ₹129–₹595, but this does not price all cosmetics, fragrance or skincare. [10](https://www.nykaa.com/makeup/eyes/kajal/c/687)
- Meesho adult jutti listings showed examples ₹314–₹1,564, some conditional on first-order offers; these do not substantiate all shoes. [11](https://meesho.com/hi/jutti/pl/ybkys)
- Rubans oxidised jhumka with hair chains was observed at ₹654, not a universal jewellery price. [12](https://www.myntra.com/earrings/rubans/rubans-oxidised-silver-plated-ghumroo-beaded-dome-jhumka-with-triple-layer-hair-chain/25417260/buy)

**Pricing acceptance is not complete.** Remaining IDs and prices are recorded in `docs/audits/price-mapping.json`.
